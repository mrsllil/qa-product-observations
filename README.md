
# QA & Product Observations

A collection of bugs and product improvement ideas based on my everyday experience with real applications.

I'm Klaudia, a QA Engineer with over 7 years of experience in software testing, requirements analysis, and identifying risks early in the development process.

Working in QA has shaped the way I interact with software. Even outside of work, I often notice unexpected behavior, inconsistencies, confusing workflows, or small details that could make an application easier to use.

I created this repository to document some of these observations and share how I approach software quality from both a technical and a user's perspective.

## What you'll find here

This repository contains two types of observations:

- **Bugs** — Unexpected application behavior, functional issues, and inconsistencies between actual and expected results.
- **Improvements** — Suggestions that could make existing features more intuitive, useful, or reliable.

Each observation is documented as a GitHub Issue, with a focus on the problem, its impact on users, and the expected or suggested behavior.

Where relevant, I also include reproduction steps, screenshots, and acceptance criteria.

## My approach

I believe good QA is not only about finding defects but also about understanding why something might go wrong and how it affects the product as a whole.

I pay particular attention to:

- Identifying risks and potential issues early
- Understanding user needs and real-world scenarios
- Recognizing gaps and inconsistencies in application behavior
- Considering dependencies between features and processes
- Suggesting improvements that address actual user problems

My goal is to describe each observation clearly and provide enough context to understand why it matters.

## A note about this repository

These are independent observations based on my personal use of publicly available applications.

They are not official bug reports, and I am not affiliated with the companies or development teams behind these products.

Some issues may be specific to a particular device, application version, or environment. Where the exact cause is unknown, I avoid making assumptions.
